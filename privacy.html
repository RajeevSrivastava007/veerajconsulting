Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026 · Veeraj Consulting · rajeev@veerajconsulting.com

Veeraj Consulting ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. It applies to this website, our booking portal and our consulting engagements.

01. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, company and any details you share when you book a session, contact us or work with us.
  • Booking and payment information: collected and processed by Zoho Bookings and our payment provider. We do not store your card or bank details.
  • Engagement information: business information you share during sessions or projects.
  • Basic technical information: such as browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider for security and performance.

02. How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and schedule sessions
  • To deliver the services you have booked
  • To send invoices, receipts and service-related messages
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations
  • To reference engagements in case studies, as described in Clause 06 of our Terms of Service, unless you opt out

03. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate — such as Zoho (bookings, email, payments and business applications) and our website host — and where required by law.

04. Data storage and security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and limit access to those who need it.

05. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law (for example, for tax and accounting records).

06. Your rights

Under applicable data protection laws, including the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent for its use. Write to rajeev@veerajconsulting.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

07. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our booking portal and embedded services may use cookies needed for them to work; please see their own policies.

08. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date.
