Frequently asked questions

What does Zoho People implementation with Veeraj Consulting include?

We start with a structured discovery of how your business works today — where data lives, who touches it and where the friction sits. Then we configure Zoho People around those processes, migrate your data, automate repetitive steps and document everything so your own team can run it.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work with growing businesses across the UK, Europe, the US and India, delivered remotely, with on-site work available where needed.

How long does a Zoho People project take?

It depends on scope. Small, well-defined set-ups move quickly; multi-app rollouts are delivered in agile sprints so you see working results early. We confirm timelines after a free discovery call.

Can you fix or audit an existing Zoho People setup?

Yes. Our Zoho Health Check is a 60-minute audit that identifies what is broken, what is missing and the highest-impact fixes, followed by a written report and prioritised roadmap within 48 hours.
