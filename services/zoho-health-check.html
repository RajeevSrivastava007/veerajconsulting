Zoho Health Check: a 60-minute audit of your Zoho setup

The Zoho Health Check is a 60-minute audit of your existing Zoho CRM, Books, Inventory or Desk setup, followed by a written report and a prioritised fix list within 48 hours. It is for owners, CFOs and operations heads of growing businesses who already use Zoho but feel it is not working as well as it should. You get a clear view of what is broken, what is missing and what to fix first, for a fixed price of ₹9,999.

What's included

Everything you get from the Health Check

60-minute review call

A working session on your live Zoho account with someone from your team who knows how it is used today. We look at real records, real workflows and real reports, not a slide deck.

Written report in 48 hours

A plain-English report of what we found: what is working, what is broken, what is missing and the risks we see in how data and processes are set up.

Prioritised fix list

The highest-impact fixes, ordered by business value and effort, so you know exactly where to start. Each item says what to change and why it matters.

Short video walkthrough

A recorded walkthrough of the key findings so your team can review them in their own time and share them with anyone who missed the call.

Quick wins you can do now

Changes your own admin can make straight away, often small settings or layout fixes that remove daily friction for your team.

No lock-in

The report is yours to keep and use however you like: with your own team, with us, or with another partner.

Who it's for

Signs your Zoho setup needs a health check

The Health Check is built for businesses already running on Zoho. It suits owners and CFOs of MSMEs in India, and growing companies in the UK and the US who want an independent view before investing further.

  • Your team keeps a spreadsheet next to Zoho because they do not trust the data
  • Reports in CRM and Books do not match, and someone reconciles them by hand
  • Workflows or automations fire at the wrong time, or not at all
  • Duplicate contacts, accounts or items keep appearing
  • People have more access than they need, or not enough to do their job
  • An integration was set up years ago and nobody is sure what it does
  • The person who configured Zoho has left the business
  • You are about to add new apps or users and want a clean base first

What we check

Six areas we review in every Health Check

Data quality

Duplicates, missing fields, inconsistent picklists and records that no longer match how you work.

Workflows

Whether your sales, finance, stock and support processes in Zoho reflect what your team actually does.

Automations

Workflow rules, Blueprints, scheduled functions and Deluge scripts: what runs, what fails and what overlaps.

User roles

Roles, profiles and sharing rules, so people see what they need and nothing they should not.

Integrations

Connections between Zoho apps and third-party tools, including sync direction and error handling.

Reports

Dashboards and reports your managers rely on, and whether the numbers behind them can be trusted.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Zoho Health Check?

A Zoho Health Check is a 60-minute audit of your existing Zoho setup. We review data quality, workflows, automations, user roles, integrations and reports, then send you a written report and a prioritised fix list within 48 hours.

Which Zoho apps does the Health Check cover?

It covers the apps most businesses rely on day to day: Zoho CRM, Zoho Books, Zoho Inventory and Zoho Desk. If you use other Zoho apps, tell us when you book and we will agree the focus for the session.

How much does the Zoho Health Check cost?

The Health Check costs ₹9,999 for the 60-minute session, the written report and the prioritised fix list. The price is fixed and paid when you book.

Do you fix the problems you find?

The Health Check itself is an audit. If you want us to make the fixes, we can scope an optional fix sprint based on the report. You are free to use the report with your own team or another partner.

What do we need to prepare before the call?

Very little. Have someone with admin access to your Zoho account on the call, and note the two or three problems that frustrate your team most. We will ask questions and look at the setup together.
