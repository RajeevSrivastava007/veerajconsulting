Zoho One implementation

Zoho One implementation, planned around how your business works

Zoho One implementation is the planned rollout of Zoho's all-in-one business suite across your sales, finance, support and HR teams. We help founders, COOs and CFOs of UK and European businesses with 10 to 200 people move onto Zoho One in phased, agile sprints, starting with the processes that matter most. Every rollout begins with how your business works, not with the software.

What Zoho One is

Zoho One in plain English

Zoho One is a single subscription that Zoho calls “the operating system for business”. It gives your business access to more than 50 Zoho applications, including CRM, accounting, support desk, HR, projects and analytics. The apps share one user directory and can share data, so sales, finance, support and HR work from the same records instead of separate tools.

Why it matters

When customer, invoice and support data live in one connected system, your team stops copying information between tools, and your reports finally agree with each other.

When it makes sense

Zoho One or individual apps?

We compare both options against your processes and user numbers before you commit to a licence.

Zoho One usually makes sense when

  • Several teams need several apps that should share data
  • You are replacing a patchwork of tools with one connected system
  • You expect to add more processes, such as HR or projects, within a year
  • You want one vendor, one login and one admin console

Individual apps may be better when

  • You only need one or two apps, such as CRM or Books
  • Only a small part of the team will use Zoho
  • You plan to keep other systems for most of your processes
  • You want to prove value with one team before committing further

UK specifics

Set up for UK businesses

VAT and Making Tax Digital

Since April 2022, every VAT-registered UK business must keep digital VAT records and file returns through HMRC-recognised software. Zoho Books is MTD-compliant, so returns can be filed from Books. We configure VAT rates, tax settings and invoice templates, and work alongside your accountant.

GBP and multi-currency

We set GBP as your base currency and configure other currencies where you trade with customers or suppliers in Europe and beyond.

GDPR-aware setup

We design roles, permissions, data retention fields and consent tracking with GDPR in mind. Your legal adviser stays responsible for compliance decisions.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a Zoho One rollout take?

It depends on how many teams and processes are involved. We plan the rollout in phases and deliver in short agile sprints, so the first apps are live early while the rest follow. We give you a timeline after the discovery phase, once we understand your processes.

Do we need all 50+ apps in Zoho One?

No. Zoho One gives you access to more than 50 applications, but most businesses start with a handful of core apps such as CRM, Books and Desk. We help you choose what to use now, what to add later and what to leave alone.

Can you migrate from HubSpot or Xero?

Yes. We plan and run migrations from tools such as HubSpot and Xero into Zoho CRM and Zoho Books, including mapping fields, cleaning data before import and checking balances and records after the move.

Is Zoho Books suitable for UK VAT and Making Tax Digital?

Yes. Making Tax Digital for VAT requires every VAT-registered UK business to keep digital VAT records and file VAT returns through HMRC-recognised software, and Zoho Books is MTD-compliant. We configure VAT rates, GBP as your base currency and your invoice templates, and we work alongside your accountant, who remains responsible for your filings.

Is Zoho One better than buying individual Zoho apps?

Not always. Zoho One usually makes sense when several teams need several apps that share data. If you only need one or two apps, individual subscriptions can cost less. We compare both options for your business before you commit.
