About Veeraj Consulting

Technology that fits the business — not the other way round.

We're a Zoho consulting firm helping small and mid-sized businesses across the UK, Europe, the US and India turn scattered processes into systems their teams can rely on.

Our story

Why we started

Too many growing businesses buy software first and fit their processes around it later. The result is licences that go unused, workflows that break, and teams that quietly go back to spreadsheets.

Veeraj Consulting was set up to reverse that order. Every engagement begins with understanding how the business actually runs — then we choose, configure and automate the tools that support it.

Veeraj Consulting was founded on 1 April 2026, bringing more than a decade of hands-on Zoho experience into a firm built around one simple idea: understand the business first, then build the system.

What we believe

Good systems are invisible. Your team should spend its time on customers and growth, not on chasing data across tools. If we've done our job well, you won't need us to keep things running.

How we work

Principles behind every engagement

Business first

We map your processes before touching any configuration — where data lives, who uses it and where time is lost.

Technology-agnostic

We fit solutions to your existing landscape. If something other than Zoho suits a need better, we'll say so.

Agile delivery

Work is delivered in short, continuous cycles, so you see progress early and can change direction without waste.

No lock-in

Everything is documented and handed over, so your own team can maintain and extend it confidently.
