Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2026 · Veeraj Consulting · rajeev@veerajconsulting.com

Consultation sessions are delivered on a time and expertise basis. Once a session is booked and paid for, the slot is reserved exclusively for you and preparation begins. Please read this policy before booking.

01. All sales are final

All paid sessions are professional services. Once payment is confirmed, the slot is reserved and preparation begins, so all payments are final and non-refundable.

02. No refunds

No refunds are issued once payment is processed, including for change of mind, non-attendance, partial use of the session, dissatisfaction with the advice given, technical difficulties on the client's side, or any reason not covered by Section 05.

03. No cancellations

Sessions cannot be cancelled once paid. No credits, vouchers, partial refunds or other compensation are provided for cancelled sessions.

04. Rescheduling

You may request a one-time reschedule by writing to rajeev@veerajconsulting.com at least 24 hours before the session. Rescheduling is subject to availability and is not guaranteed; if no suitable slot is found, no refund is due. If you do not attend without prior notice, the session is treated as fully delivered.

05. If we cannot conduct the session

If Veeraj Consulting is unable to conduct a confirmed session, we will proactively offer a rescheduled session at no additional cost. This is the sole remedy and does not entitle the client to a refund.

06. Free discovery call

The free 30-minute "Let's Just Talk" discovery call involves no payment, so this policy does not apply to it. You may reschedule or skip it without any obligation.

Questions?

Write to rajeev@veerajconsulting.com before booking if anything in this policy is unclear.

Questions before you book?

Write to us or start with a free 30-minute call — we're happy to clarify anything in advance.
